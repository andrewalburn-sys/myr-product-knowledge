# 02 Magical Vs Mechanical

## Purpose

Separate table-stakes utilities from high-differentiation intelligence moments so MyRecipes knows where AI should stay mostly invisible and where it should become transformational.

## Why It Matters Here

Cooking products contain a mix of basic utilities and emotionally meaningful moments. If MyRecipes treats all of them as equal AI opportunities, the result will be noisy, over-labeled, and strategically weak. This framework helps reserve AI ambition for the parts of the experience where intelligence actually changes the feeling and outcome.

## How To Use It

1. List core tasks across the cooking journey.
2. Mark which are basic functional expectations versus which create confidence, delight, or relief.
3. Decide whether AI should be hidden, ambient, or explicit in each case.
4. Use the results to guide product design, not just prioritization.

## Inputs

- Journey stages and pain density
- Persona trust profiles
- Existing MyRecipes opportunities
- Competitive expectations for basic recipe utilities

## Evaluation Lens

For each task, ask:

- Is this expected table-stakes behavior?
- Does success here feel magical or merely competent?
- Would visible AI improve the experience or make it feel heavier?
- Is the differentiation in the outcome or in the interface?

## MyRecipes Application

### Mechanical Functions

These must work well, but are not the primary place to differentiate through visible AI:

| Function | Why it is mechanical | Best AI posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recipe storage and saving | Baseline product expectation | AI can work underneath to adapt or organize, but should not call attention to itself |
| Grocery list creation | Useful but expected | Use AI to consolidate and clean up behind the scenes |
| Pantry sync and ingredient matching | Important utility | Show practical outputs, not AI theater |
| Timers and step progression | Core kitchen utility | Lightweight and reliable beats clever |
| Quantity scaling and right-sizing | Functional need | Use intelligence quietly to improve accuracy |

### Magical Moments

These are the moments where MyRecipes can feel meaningfully more intelligent than existing cooking products:

| Moment | Why it is magical | Best AI posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What can I cook with what is already here? | Converts uncertainty into an actionable answer | Ambient intelligence |
| Tell me the one dinner that fits tonight | Removes decision burden under stress | Contextual decision engine |
| Is this actually worth making? | Turns abundance into conviction | Curator with trust signals |
| Help me pace multiple dishes without stress | Feels like a real assistant in the kitchen | Explicit guidance if requested |
| Something changed, fix the plan | Recovery is where current tools break | Explicit rescue or rebalancing engine |
| Make the product feel like it knows me | Personalization that compounds silently | Invisible AI |

### MyRecipes Design Rule

For MyRecipes, the right posture is:

- `Mechanical`: AI is mostly invisible and expressed as product quality
- `Magical`: AI is allowed to be obvious only when it creates a meaningful shift in confidence, speed, or resilience

This is especially important because the largest segment, the Established Home Cook, is skeptical of explicit AI but still benefits from silent intelligence.

## Strategic Implications

- Visible AI should be concentrated in high-value moments, not across every workflow.
- The product should feel smarter overall, but not more complicated.
- Mechanical quality is the baseline; magical outcomes are the differentiator.
- The brand should avoid AI theater and instead invest in high-trust intelligence moments.

## Decision Prompts

- Is this a moment where the user needs utility or relief?
- Would a visible AI layer feel helpful or performative here?
- If we removed the AI label, would the feature still feel meaningfully better?
- Is this moment important enough to justify a more explicit AI interaction?

## Output Artifact

A classified map of MyRecipes experiences showing where AI should be invisible, ambient, or explicit.
