# 05 TenX Vs TenPercent Test

## Purpose

Pressure-test AI opportunities to distinguish minor improvements from step-function product shifts.

## Why It Matters Here

Cooking is full of places where AI could make things slightly nicer. That is not enough. MyRecipes should invest heavily only where intelligence creates a qualitatively different outcome: less thinking, less failure, less rebuilding, or more confidence than users can get today.

## How To Use It

1. List each AI opportunity under consideration.
2. Describe the user outcome if it works.
3. Compare that outcome to current behavior and alternatives.
4. Classify the idea as `10%`, `2x`, or `10x`.
5. Use the classification to decide whether the idea deserves major investment, limited support, or deprioritization.

## Inputs

- Opportunity set
- Pain density findings
- JTBD matrix
- Moments of truth ranking

## Evaluation Lens

Ask these questions:

- Does this remove a meaningful step or just streamline one?
- Does it reduce cognitive load, failure risk, or time-to-outcome in a visible way?
- Would the user materially change behavior if this existed?
- Is the improvement specific to AI, or could normal UX achieve most of it?

## MyRecipes Application

### Opportunity Pressure Test

| Opportunity | Likely impact | Why | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Constraint-first search | `2x to 10x` | For Weeknight Reducer, collapsing many variables into one answer changes the core decision moment | High priority |
| Persistent preference memory | `2x` | Powerful as infrastructure, but usually felt over time rather than as a dramatic moment | Build as foundation |
| Repertoire-aware novelty scoring | `2x to 10x` | For Established Home Cook and Enthusiast, meaningful novelty can change trust and engagement | High priority |
| Pantry-aware discovery | `2x` | Strong practical value, especially under time pressure | High priority if pantry data quality is credible |
| Constraint-adaptive plan generation | `10x` | Replaces a manual optimization workflow for the System Thinker | Major strategic bet |
| Plan-consolidated shopping | `2x to 10x` | If accurate, it materially reduces admin work and planning fragility | High priority |
| AI kitchen companion | `2x to 10x` | Can be transformational in execution, but only if context is strong and interaction is low-friction | Focused investment |
| Substitution engine | `2x` | Very valuable in high-stress moments, but narrower than planning or decision support | Important support layer |
| Step-aware hands-free guide | `2x` | Improves execution confidence, especially for select personas | Good differentiator |
| Plan recovery engine | `10x` | Existing tools rarely rebalance a week after disruption | Major strategic bet |

### What Counts As 10x For MyRecipes

For this product, `10x` means one of the following:

- the user no longer has to solve the problem manually,
- the user no longer has to restart when things change,
- the user can trust the answer without doing extra validation,
- or the product eliminates a major category of stress.

### What Counts As Only 10%

These are ideas that may be useful but should not anchor the strategy:

- lightly improved recommendations with no stronger confidence signal
- AI-generated tips that do not change the outcome
- generic conversational overlays that add explanation but not resolution
- novelty for its own sake without better fit or trust

## Strategic Implications

- MyRecipes should place major bets on decision collapse, week planning, and recovery.
- Foundational capabilities matter even when they do not feel like 10x on their own because they enable the real 10x moments.
- Many surface-level AI ideas should be deprioritized unless they support a larger strategic bet.

## Decision Prompts

- If this feature disappeared, would the user still behave the same way?
- Does this remove work or just make the work slightly nicer?
- Is this feature transformative for a major persona or only interesting in demos?
- Does this opportunity justify cross-functional investment?

## Output Artifact

A prioritization table that separates major AI bets, enabling capabilities, and lower-value ideas.
