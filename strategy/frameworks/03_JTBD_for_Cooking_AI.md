# 03 Jobs To Be Done For Cooking AI

## Purpose

Define the real jobs users are hiring MyRecipes to do so product strategy is grounded in outcomes, not in feature ideas.

## Why It Matters Here

MyRecipes is at risk of being framed as a recipe product with AI features layered on top. JTBD reframes the opportunity: users are not hiring the app to "browse recipes." They are hiring it to reduce decision load, avoid failure, and feel more capable across the cooking journey.

## How To Use It

1. Write the user's job in outcome language.
2. Document what tool or workaround they use today.
3. Describe why the current solution fails.
4. Identify what AI could uniquely do better.
5. Determine whether the job is core to retention, trust, or differentiation.

## Inputs

- Persona pain points and goals
- Current alternatives such as Google, TikTok, saved recipes, grocery lists, timers, and mental planning
- Opportunity set by journey stage

## Evaluation Lens

For each job, assess:

- `Urgency`: how pressing the job feels in the moment
- `Importance`: how central the job is to the user's success
- `Current failure`: how badly current solutions fail
- `AI advantage`: whether intelligence can materially improve the outcome
- `Differentiation potential`: whether solving the job makes MyRecipes feel distinct

## MyRecipes Application

### Core Jobs Across The Journey

| Job to be done | Primary personas | Current solution | Why it fails | AI opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Help me decide what to cook quickly | Weeknight Reducer, Established Home Cook | Search, browsing, memory | Too many options, not enough relevance or confidence | Narrow to one strong answer with visible reasoning |
| Help me build a week that works | System Thinker, Weeknight Reducer | Spreadsheets, lists, mental planning, multiple apps | Manual optimization across budget, nutrition, preferences, overlap, and schedule | Generate and maintain a coherent week plan |
| Help me know I can actually pull this off | Weeknight Reducer, Enthusiast | Manual pantry check, recipe reading, separate shopping tools | Feasibility is hidden until too late | Pantry-aware filtering, right-sized quantities, pre-resolved substitutions |
| Help me trust this dish is worth the effort | Enthusiast, Established Home Cook | Editorial copy, ratings, comments, intuition | Conviction signals are weak or generic | Surface UGC proof, provenance, novelty fit, and confidence context |
| Help me not mess this up while cooking | Weeknight Reducer, Enthusiast | Static recipe, timers, memory | Recipes do not adapt to pace, confusion, or mistakes | Step-aware guidance and technique help on demand |
| Help me recover when things go wrong | Weeknight Reducer, System Thinker, Enthusiast | Manual improvisation, abandonment, takeout | Existing tools have no recovery path | Substitution engine and plan recovery engine |
| Help me improve without extra work | All personas | Saved recipes, memory, ad hoc notes | Learning does not compound over time | Preference memory and behavioral feedback loop |

### Job Hierarchy For MyRecipes

These jobs are not equal. For product strategy, organize them into three levels:

#### Core Decision Jobs

- Help me decide what to make
- Help me build a realistic plan
- Help me know this will work

These are the front door to the product's value.

#### Core Execution Jobs

- Help me cook without stress
- Help me recover when something breaks

These are where trust is either won or lost.

#### Core Learning Jobs

- Help the product understand me better over time
- Help future decisions get easier

These are what turn AI from a feature into a compounding system.

## Strategic Implications

- MyRecipes should be framed as a `decision + execution companion`, not a recipe destination.
- The product promise should shift from "we have recipes" to "we help you get dinner right."
- Planning and recovery are not support functions; they are central jobs for high-value personas.
- Discovery should increasingly serve downstream confidence, not just content consumption.

## Decision Prompts

- What job is the user actually hiring us to do in this moment?
- Is the user looking for options, reassurance, orchestration, or rescue?
- Is our current answer solving the user's real job or just presenting content?
- If we solved this job perfectly, would the user change behavior?

## Output Artifact

A JTBD matrix that clarifies MyRecipes' product identity, highest-value user outcomes, and the jobs AI is best suited to own.
