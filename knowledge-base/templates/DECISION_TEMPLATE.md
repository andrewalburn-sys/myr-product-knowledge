# Decision template

```markdown
## ID — Short decision title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded | open

**Owner:** Andrew Alburn

**Affected sources:** `path/to/source.md`

**Context:** What question or conflict required a decision?

**Decision:** What is now true?

**Rationale:** Why was this choice made?

**Consequences:** What must change, what stays unchanged, and what tradeoff was accepted?

**Supersedes:** Decision ID or `none`.
```
